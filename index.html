RP203 Setup Guide

Plug-and-play setup for Windows and macOS

🪟 Windows (10 & 11) — Plug & Play:

  1. Plug your USB-to-TTL adapter into any USB port. Windows auto-assigns it (e.g. COM3 or COM4).
  2. Open this app in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  3. Click Printer → Connect Printer, select your USB Serial device from the popup, and you're ready!

🍎 macOS Setup:

If your CH340 adapter doesn't show in Chrome, run in terminal:

brew install --cask wch-ch34x-usb-serial-driver

Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, click Allow, and restart your Mac.
